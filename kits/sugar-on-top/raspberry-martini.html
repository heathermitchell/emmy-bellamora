The palette

Eight colors. Eight jobs.

Two darks. Two lights. One neutral. Three hues. Each color knows the one job no other color does. Seven are cool. One is warm.

  • 1 · Anchor

    Velvet Anchor

    #13141A

    Deepest cool dark. Identity at rest. The slate floor, the dark silk of the bar back.

    Highlight Tint text on Velvet Anchor · 17.58:1 AAA

  • 2 · Ink

    Ink Slate

    #3A3E4A

    Reading dark. Body text on light grounds. The concrete wall at distance, in shadow.

    Ink Slate text on Highlight Tint · 10.21:1 AAA

  • 3 · Counter

    Cool Jade

    #377067

    The second hue. Captions, secondary UI, the room’s second voice.

    Cool Jade text on Highlight Tint · 5.46:1 AA

  • 4 · Signature

    Raspberry Martini

    #CE2B59

    The ONE warm thing in the room. The drink in her hand, lit from across the bar.

    Highlight Tint text on Raspberry Martini · 4.88:1 AA · Raspberry Martini text on Velvet Anchor, large only · 3.60:1

  • 5 · Bridge

    Bridge Mist

    #9F9295

    Borders, dividers, metadata. It whispers the raspberry’s hue-family without saying it aloud.

    Bridge Mist text on Velvet Anchor · 6.14:1 AA

The materials

This kit isn’t dressed like a cocktail bar. It is one.

The walls are concrete. The bar surface is chrome and slate. Gin-clear ice in the glass. And one raspberry.

  • Chrome

    The bar surface. The glint off the shaker.

  • Slate

    The slate floor, the dark silk of the bar back.

  • Gin-clear ice

    Hard-edged and clear. The rim catching light.

  • Concrete

    The walls. Cool, matte, and in shadow at the far end of the room.

  • Raspberry

    Solid, never a wash. The raspberry is placed; it doesn’t bleed.
