The palette

Eight colors. Eight jobs.

Dark depth lifted by champagne pink. This is Velvet Noir.

Two darks, one bridge, one plum, one pink, one gold, and two neutrals at opposite temperatures: warm linen inside, cool pearl at the window. No two colors do the same job.

    Velvet Drape

The materials

Nothing is flat. Everything is material.

Every swatch should make you want to touch it.

  • Crushed velvet

    Deep saturation with a subtle directional pile. Smoky Plum.

  • Dark chocolate

    Matte, with grain. The outside of the truffle box. Dark Ganache.

  • Champagne foil

    Metallic and reflective. The ribbon catching candlelight. Champagne Foil.

  • Pearl and marble

    Cool gleam, subtle, never flat. The marble writing desk. Pearl Light.

  • Linen paper

    The tissue in the gift box, cream cardstock, the cookbook page. Cocoa Cream.

  • Midnight glass

    The night sky on the window, the faintest blue-violet. Velvet Drape.
