The palette

Every color
at full volume.

Nine colors. Four accents. A maximalist-track palette built for the woman who doesn't apologize for taking up space — chromatically or otherwise. The brights attract. The darks ground. The cream breathes. The concrete holds the cool.

Anchor

Tangerine Fizz

#FF6B35

Fresh-squeezed orange with sparkling water. Party orange. The dopamine-warm identity of the woman who wears the orange.

Counterweight

Fuchsia Shock

#E8356D

Hot pink that didn't ask permission. The neon sign in the window. Confident without trying.

Flex Hue

Lime Volt

#B8E62E

The surprise ingredient. Citrus meets neon. The thing that makes the palette pop instead of just "bright."

Warm Carrier

Sherbet Cream

#FFF0E0

The palate cleanser. Warm cream with the faintest peach blush. Breathing room that holds the warm register.

Cool Carrier

Dusk Concrete

#8C8896

Rooftop concrete at golden hour — warm light hits it and it still reads cool. The quiet surface the palette was missing.

Accent

Lemon Zap

#FFD23F

Sunshine concentrated. The fizz at the top of the drink. Used in punctuation moments — call-outs, hover states.

Accent

Raspberry Velvet

#8B1A4A

Where the party gets intimate. Fuchsia's older sister — she's been here before and she's staying late.

Deepest Dark

Midnight Mango

#2A1525

Not black. Black would kill this palette. Warm, fruity even in shadow. Where the eye rests in the absence of light.

Cool Ground

Twilight Grape

#4A2060

Deep purple that gives the brights somewhere dramatic to stand. The sky behind the rooftop going dark.
