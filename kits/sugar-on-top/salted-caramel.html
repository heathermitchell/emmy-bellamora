Salted Caramel — Sugar on Top

On the Salt

“Every warm palette needs a moment of cool contrast —
or it collapses into mud.”

This is why we named the kit Salted Caramel, not just Caramel. Garden Sage and Stone Counter aren’t decorations — they’re the structural counterweight that keeps the warm palette from going flat.

“The salt that makes the caramel” is both a flavor principle and a brand principle. A palette that’s all warmth, all the time, has nowhere to go. The contrast — the cool herb, the marble slab — is what makes the warmth feel like warmth, and not just beige.

When you use this kit, let Garden Sage do its job. Put it on the windowsill of your Instagram grid. Let Stone Counter carry your coolest section backgrounds. The warmth will sing louder for having somewhere to land between notes.
