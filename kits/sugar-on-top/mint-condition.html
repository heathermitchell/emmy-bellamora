What this kit feels like

Clean isn't cold.
It's focused.

Mint Condition is built around one truth: green is the easiest color for the human eye to process. Add one warm earth tone (Aloe Honey) and you don't have a clinical palette — you have a spa. A greenhouse. A window seat with good light.

Eight colors, all with personality. Eight textures, all from nature. Fraunces headlines that feel cultivated, not cold. Lora italic for the sentences you want people to slow down for. Manrope that disappears — so the colors can do the talking.
